    ESRS Navigator Stakeholder Map

    Home / Archimate / ESRS Navigator Stakeholder Map

    Stakeholder map based on the ESRS Navigator document for sustainability reporting under CSRD

    Diagrams

    • ESRS Stakeholder Overview (Logical)

      This logical diagram provides a comprehensive overview of all stakeholders in the ESRS reporting ecosystem, including companies, employees, suppliers, customers, and various organizations that interact with the reporting organization. It highlights the different types of stakeholders, their roles, and responsibilities, and how they are connected within the ecosystem. The diagram is intended to provide a high-level view of the stakeholders involved in the ESRS reporting process.

    Elements

    • Stakeholder Large Companies (>250 employees)

      Companies with over 250 employees are required to report under CSRD from 2025.

    • Stakeholder FreshFood B.V. Example company: 450 employees, food production, two facilities in Netherlands
    • Stakeholder Listed SMEs Small and medium enterprises with stock exchange listing (future LSME standard)
    • Stakeholder Non-listed SMEs SMEs that voluntarily report (future VSME standard)
    • BusinessRole Board of Directors (Directie) CEO, CFO, COO - Final responsibility for sustainability policy
    • BusinessRole CEO Chief Executive Officer
    • BusinessRole CFO Chief Financial Officer - Often responsible for compliance
    • BusinessRole COO Chief Operating Officer
    • BusinessRole Supervisory Board (RvC) Oversight and quarterly reporting on sustainability
    • BusinessRole Sustainability Manager/Director Coordinates implementation of sustainability measures, reports to CEO
    • BusinessRole Operations Manager Responsible for water policy and production facilities
    • BusinessRole Sustainability Committee Committee supporting sustainability initiatives
    • BusinessRole Works Council (Ondernemingsraad) Employee representation with quarterly management consultation
    • Stakeholder Own Personnel (ESRS S1) All employees of the reporting organization
    • Stakeholder Permanent Employees Full-time permanent staff
    • Stakeholder Seasonal Workers Temporary/seasonal employees
    • Stakeholder Production Workers Workers in production facilities
    • Stakeholder Suppliers All upstream suppliers in the value chain
    • Stakeholder Agricultural Suppliers Tomato growers, grain producers, vegetable suppliers
    • Stakeholder Packaging Suppliers Providers of packaging materials
    • Stakeholder Transport Companies Logistics and transportation providers
    • Stakeholder Workers in Value Chain (ESRS S2) Employees of suppliers and partners
    • Stakeholder Seasonal Agricultural Workers Seasonal workers at supplier farms
    • Stakeholder Customers Direct business customers
    • Stakeholder Supermarket Chains Retail customers
    • Stakeholder Foodservice Companies B2B foodservice customers
    • Stakeholder Consumers and End Users (ESRS S4) Final consumers - focus on food safety and product information
    • Stakeholder Affected Communities (ESRS S3) Local and regional communities impacted by operations
    • Stakeholder Local Communities Communities around production facilities and supplier regions
    • Stakeholder Indigenous Peoples Indigenous communities where applicable
    • Stakeholder Investors/Shareholders Users of sustainability statements for investment decisions
    • Stakeholder Banks and Financial Institutions Financial service providers and creditors
    • Stakeholder Credit Rating Agencies Agencies assessing creditworthiness including ESG factors
    • Stakeholder European Commission Issues CSRD directive and ESRS regulations
    • Stakeholder EFRAG European Financial Reporting Advisory Group - develops ESRS standards
    • Stakeholder Rijksdienst voor Ondernemen (RVO) Dutch government agency - MVO-steunpunt provides CSR guidance
    • Stakeholder AFM Autoriteit Financiële Markten - Financial market supervision
    • Stakeholder Ministry of Economic Affairs Commissioned the ESRS Navigator
    • Stakeholder Sociaal Economische Raad (SER) Provides information and webinars on CSRD/ESRS
    • Stakeholder External Auditors Must approve sustainability reporting
    • Stakeholder Certification Bodies ISO certifications and verification services
    • Stakeholder Industry Associations Sector-specific organizations like FNLI
    • Stakeholder NGOs and Civil Society Non-governmental organizations providing consultation
    • Stakeholder Trade Unions Worker unions in production regions
    • Stakeholder Legal Design Agencies Agencies like Patroon Legal Design
    • Stakeholder Sustainability Consultants External advisors and food safety experts
    • Stakeholder Research and Analysis Firms Verification bureaus for water, energy, CO2 data
    • Stakeholder Chamber of Commerce/Kamer van Koophandel Chamber of Commerce - provides CSRD guidance
    • Stakeholder Training Providers Food safety, compliance, and sustainability training
    • BusinessObject ESRS E1 - Climate Climate change reporting standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS E2 - Pollution Pollution reporting standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS E3 - Water Water and marine resources standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS E4 - Biodiversity Biodiversity and ecosystems standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS E5 - Circular Economy Material use and circular economy standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS S1 - Own Personnel Own workforce standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS S2 - Value Chain Workers Workers in the value chain standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS S3 - Communities Affected communities standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS S4 - Consumers Consumers and end-users standard
    • BusinessObject ESRS G1 - Business Conduct Business conduct standard
